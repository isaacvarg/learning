# Mission: Morse code

## Why
A personal challenge: to read and send Morse code fluently at 20 wpm, by ear and by hand, for the satisfaction of mastering it.

## Success looks like
- Copy plain-text Morse at 20 wpm (20 wpm character and effective speed) with 90% character accuracy
- Send plain text at 20 wpm on keyboard or screen tap that a decoder reads with 90% accuracy
- Recognise all 26 letters, 10 digits and common punctuation (`. , ? / =`) instantly, by sound

## Constraints
- 20-30 minutes of practice a day
- Sending on keyboard or screen tap; no hardware key
- No deadline

## Out of scope
- Ham radio licensing, on-air operating, Q-codes and QSO procedure
- Light or visual Morse, non-Latin Morse alphabets
